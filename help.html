Cane Race Help

Welcome to Cane Race, an endless runner type game, similar to mainstream games like temple run and Subway Surfer but a different kind of deal.

In this game you are running on a street, dodging people with canes and people on skateboards,collect coins along the way and survive for as long as you can! Every 50 points reached grands you another level, making you and the obstacles faster!

Cars standing on the street spawn randomly. When you start walking onto it you have exactly 4 steps on the car's roof, the 5th one makes you fall off, stunning you for 3 seconds where you could get hit by a skateboard...

The total coins you picked up are displayed below the "Play Game" button, they currently have no use but I plan to add a shop where you can buy upgrades as well in the near future.

The score, also displayed under your total coins resets whenever you start a new game.

Levelling up doesn't have any sound yet, but your screenreader informs you about it and your braille display as well since a score increase, getting stunned or levelling up is printed out onto the screen.

Keys

  • Uparrow/Spacebar: jump
  • Left Arrow: turn left
  • Right Arrow: turn right

Conclusion

I hope you enjoy this game as much as I enjoyed making it and editing all the sounds.

Have fun!
